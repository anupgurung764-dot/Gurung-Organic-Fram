# Gurung-Organic-Fram
Gurung Organic Farm is a future-focused local farm in Gairi Gaon Busty, near Bijanbari, Darjeeling. We aim to provide fresh milk, organic vegetables, and a variety of locally grown farm products, including potatoes, beans, squash, radish, cabbage, and other seasonal produce. Our goal is to promote fresh, healthy, and natural farming. । 🌱🥛🐄
